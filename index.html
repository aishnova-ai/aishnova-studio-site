Questions

Before you buy.

What is Aishnova?

Aishnova is an AI business studio for creators and solopreneurs. Its flagship program, Claude Studio Kit, teaches you to learn, build and earn with AI. Accelerator tools like Threads Growth Studio speed up the daily work, and the free AI Workflow Lab community, 1:1 mentorship and custom builds support you along the way.

Where should I start?

Start with Claude Studio Kit. It takes you from setup to your first paying client in four stages. Add an accelerator like Threads Growth Studio when you want to save time on a specific job.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything is written for non-technical creators, with step-by-step guides and copy-ready prompts. If you can copy and paste, you can do this.

Is it a subscription?

No. Claude Studio Kit and the accelerators are one-time payments. You pay once and keep them, with updates included.

What do I need to run the tools?

A Claude account. Most tools run inside Claude as a skill or plugin, and a paid Claude plan gives the best results.

What is AI Workflow Lab?

AI Workflow Lab (AWL) is the free Aishnova community on Skool. Members get free AI resources, workflows and support for building with Claude.

How is mentorship different from the Kit?

The Kit and the accelerators are self-paced. Mentorship is live, one-to-one time where we build a system around your own business, offer and content.
